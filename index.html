About Me

Hi, I'm Dan Michael Alfaras, an Information Technology graduate from Asia Pacific College with a strong background in web development and a passion for creating modern, responsive, and user-friendly websites that provide a smooth and engaging experience across different devices.

During my studies, I gained hands-on experience in both front-end and back-end development using technologies such as HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, Bootstrap, PHP, Laravel, WordPress, and MySQL. I also have experience with Git, Trello, and Google Analytics, which have helped me develop, manage, and monitor web projects effectively.

I enjoy turning ideas into functional digital experiences, with a focus on responsive design, intuitive navigation, clean code, usability, and accessibility. I am committed to continuous learning and enjoy exploring new technologies, frameworks, and development practices to improve my skills and create better web solutions.

My goal is to continue growing as a web developer, gain valuable professional experience, and create reliable and engaging web solutions that address real-world needs. I am always open to learning new skills, solving problems, taking on new challenges, and collaborating on projects that allow me to grow both personally and professionally.
